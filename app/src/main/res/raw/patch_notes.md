## v0.10.0

**Rearrange the weapon wheel**

Long-press any slot on the weapon wheel and drag it to a new spot. The slots in between slide along to make room, and the layout saves automatically — your pistols can live wherever you want them next time you open the wheel. GTA IV and GTA V remember their own orders, so a tweak on one game does not touch the other.

Long-press the center of the wheel to reset the layout back to the game's default order. Short taps still equip weapons like before.
