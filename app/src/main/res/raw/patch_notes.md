## v0.12.0

**Settings per game**

Every switch in Settings is now split between GTA IV and GTA V. Flipping a toggle while playing IV does not change what V does, and the other way around. The saved cheat loadout, the one-shots you long-pressed to auto-fire, the auto-reapply master switch, and which cheat section you had collapsed are all remembered per game.

The setup screen's Settings has GTA IV and GTA V tabs so you can configure either game before anything is connected. Once in-game, Settings opens straight to that game. Cheats sits above Minimap now.

**Settings panel, redesigned**

The old Settings sheet dragged open from the bottom. The drag competed with inner scroll, so a flick near the top would collapse the sheet instead of scrolling. Settings is now a full-screen slide-up with a close icon in the top right. The back button closes it too. Every other sheet in the app (radio picker, update prompt, info dialogs, cheats onboarding) picked up the same close icon for consistency, and they all slide out when you tap it instead of vanishing.
