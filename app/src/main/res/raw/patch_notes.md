## v1.0.0

NOTE: download the latest ThorBridge.asi and ThorBridgeV.asi from the release assets and drop them next to GTAIV.exe and GTA5.exe respectively. The APK update alone doesn't pick up asi changes.

**Wardrobe is here**

Hold your character name on the money card to open Wardrobe. Swap tops, pants, shoes, accessories, and props. Save looks you like and bring them back later with one tap. Works on both GTA IV and GTA V.

**Hold any card to open its shop**

Long-press is now the way to reach the shops without leaving the HUD. Hold your name for Wardrobe (both games). On GTA V, hold the weapon card on foot to open Ammu-Nation, or hold the vehicle card while driving to open LS Customs. On GTA IV, the weapon and vehicle cards show a quick note; those shops are V-only for now.

**Endless swiping**

A new Settings toggle lets you swipe past the last tab back to the first (and vice versa). Off by default; find it in Settings → HUD.
