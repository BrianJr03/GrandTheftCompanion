## v0.11.0

NOTE: new `ThorBridge.asi` and `ThorBridgeV.asi` ship with this release. Download them from the release assets and drop them next to your `GTAIV.exe` / `GTA5.exe`. The APK alone does not pick up the plugin changes.

**Cheats tab**

A new third tab on the bottom panel next to hud and map. Grouped into collapsible sections (health, wanted, weapons, vehicle, world, toggles) so related buttons stay together. Tap a section header to collapse it; everything starts open.

**GTA IV**: heal, wanted +1 and clear, all weapons, max ammo, fix vehicle, next weather, next time of day, parachute, and the phone-cheat weapon sets (advanced, poor, and the ultimate 482-555-0100 combo). Five toggles: invincible, fast reload, never tired, slow motion, police ignore.

**GTA V**: everything IV has that applies, plus wanted -1, fill special, skyfall, and six spawn-car buttons (adder, zentorno, bati, sanchez, frogger, bmx). Thirteen toggles including infinite clip, fast run, low gravity, drunk, fast swim, super jump, explosive ammo, explosive melee, incendiary ammo, and slippery cars.

Toggle buttons light from what the game reports, not from the tap, so a toggle the game refuses never looks on. The last refusal shows as a short red line at the bottom.

## v0.10.0

**Rearrange the weapon wheel**

Long-press any slot on the weapon wheel and drag it to a new spot. The slots in between slide along to make room, and the layout saves automatically — your pistols can live wherever you want them next time you open the wheel. GTA IV and GTA V remember their own orders, so a tweak on one game does not touch the other.

Long-press the center of the wheel to reset the layout back to the game's default order. Short taps still equip weapons like before.
