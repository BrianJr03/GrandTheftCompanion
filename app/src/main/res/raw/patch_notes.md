## v0.5.0

**Weapon wheel**

An eight-slot weapon wheel on the bottom screen, for both GTA IV and GTA V. Tap the weapon card to open it, or hold LB / L1 on the pad. Tap a slot to switch weapon; tap the selected slot again to step through its other weapons.

**GTA IV never had a weapon wheel in-game** — now it does, carrying every weapon from the base game, The Lost and Damned, and The Ballad of Gay Tony.

**GTA V** keeps its own wheel on the top screen; this is the touch-friendly mirror on the bottom.

**TLAD and TBoGT weapons by name**

Weapons from the two episodes now show their actual name on the HUD and in the wheel (assault SMG, sticky bomb, grenade launcher, pipe bomb, and so on) instead of the "episodic N" placeholder they used before. The id-11 shotgun also now correctly reads "combat shotgun" instead of "sawed-off shotgun".

## v0.4.0

**First-run swipe hint**

A small "Tip" dialog appears the first time you connect, pointing out the HUD and the mirrored minimap pages. It shows once and is remembered.

**Minimap mirror polish**

The HUD and map tabs no longer have labels at the top; just swipe to switch. The pinch-to-zoom and drag-to-pan hint moved to the corner of the map page and only appears while you're actively adjusting.

**Pause takes over both pages**

When GTA IV is paused, or GTA V shows an on-screen notice (online, cutscene switch, pause), the whole bottom screen now shows that state instead of letting you swipe to a half-live map.

**Wanted level polish**

Thumbstick police lights are dimmer now so they're easier on the eyes at arm's length. Stars fill from the right in both GTA IV and GTA V, matching how the game draws them on the top screen.
