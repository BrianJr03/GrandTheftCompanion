## v1.1.0

NOTE: download the latest ThorBridgeV.asi from the release assets and drop it next to GTA5.exe. The APK update alone doesn't pick up asi changes. GTA IV side is unchanged, so leave ThorBridge.asi alone.

**Trainer section**

A new section on the GTA V cheats tab with the stuff a classic trainer gives you. Toggle ragdoll, invisible, infinite ammo, invincible vehicle, and pause time. Tap to clear area, spawn a random cop, boat, plane, or heli, portal in front of your camera, or teleport to your current waypoint.

**Time and weather pickers**

Slide the clock to any hour, or pick from fourteen weather presets (extra sunny, clear, clouds, rain, thunder, snow, blizzard, xmas, and more). Tap stock to hand control back to the game.

**Spawn anything**

Spawn vehicle opens the full library with search and category chips. Supers, sports, muscle, off-road, planes, helis, boats, trains. Spawn object drops props, ramps, barriers, vending machines, and crates in front of you.

**Play an animation**

Pick from a curated list. Wave, salute, dance, smoke, drink, lean on a wall, cross arms. Plays once on your character.

**Teleport library**

Thirty curated spots across Los Santos and Blaine County. Michael's house, Maze Bank rooftop, Fort Zancudo gate, Vinewood sign, Mt Chiliad peak, Paleto Bay, Diamond Casino. One tap and you're there.
